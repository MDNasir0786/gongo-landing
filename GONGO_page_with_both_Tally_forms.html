Terms & Operational Policies

1. Operating Entity: GONGO is a vehicle rental marketplace owned and operated by the business firm BHARAT-MILK, headquartered in Ashoka Garden, Bhopal, MP 462023.

2. Marketplace Model: GONGO provides digital infrastructure connecting verified riders with independent vehicle rental partners. Vehicles are pre-inspected and audited for roadworthiness.

3. Rider Requirements: Riders must possess a valid Indian Government Driving License and authentic Government ID (such as Aadhaar Card) for mandatory KYC verification before vehicle handover.

4. Security Deposits & Refunds: Refundable security deposits are handled transparently per partner listing criteria with prompt returns upon verified safe vehicle return.

5. Support & Enquiries: Reach our customer care team anytime at info@gongobike.com or hotline +91 78981 14878.

Privacy & Data Security

1. Data Confidentiality: GONGO (BHARAT-MILK) respects user privacy. Personal identification and driving license details are strictly used for verified rental facilitation and legal compliance.

2. Encrypted Handling: Booking data, identity proofs, and payment credentials are encrypted using bank-grade protocols.

3. No Third-party Selling: We do not sell or monetize personal customer details to external advertising networks.

4. Grievance & Contact: For privacy requests or questions, please email gongorental@gmail.com.
